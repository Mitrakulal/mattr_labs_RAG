## Product Description
An AI-powered PTZ camera. It pans, tilts, and zooms — and the computer vision keeps your subject tracked and perfectly framed, so one operator (or none) can run a professional-looking feed.

Status: In development.

## The Wow Is the AI, Not the Motion
Watch it lock on and follow. Anyone can show a camera panning. The point is that it finds a person and keeps them framed while you get on with things. Early hardware, on the bench today — full auto-tracking demo coming as the build progresses.

Real prototype footage and photos — the current unit, printed and assembled in-house.

## What Makes It Smart

### Auto-tracking
Locks onto a person and follows them around the room — no operator hunting for the shot.

### Auto-framing
Keeps the subject composed and centred as they move, zooming to hold the frame.

### Pan · Tilt · Zoom
Smooth motorized movement across a wide range, driven by real-time firmware.

### AI on the Edge
Vision runs on-device — low latency, no cloud round-trip, works on your network.

### Yours to Customize
Tune the tracking behaviour for your use-case — or white-label the whole thing.

## The Software, Live
Not mockups — screenshots of the current system driving a real call.

Auto-framing in a live call — with hand-raise detection flagging who wants to speak.
One-click attendance: on-device face recognition marks who's present.

## Where It Earns Its Keep
On location: an early unit running a lecture-hall pilot.

### Use Cases
1. **Live streaming for small venues** — Churches, lecture halls, local sports — one camera that follows the speaker or the play, so a volunteer (or nobody) runs the stream.
2. **Hybrid meeting rooms** — Auto-frame whoever's talking so remote participants always see the right person.
3. **Specialized monitoring** — Labs, livestock, perimeters — tracking plus alerts on what actually matters.

## Target Specs (In Development)
